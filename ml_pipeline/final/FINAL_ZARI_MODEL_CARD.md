# ZARI.ai — Production Model Card

## Model Details
- **Developer**: ZARI.ai ML Engineering Team
- **Model Architecture**: EfficientNetV2-B2 + Evidential Deep Learning (EDL) Softplus Dirichlet Head
- **Input Resolution**: 256x256 RGB
- **Supported Crops**: Tomato (13 classes), Potato (3 supported classes), Pepper (6 classes)
- **Hardware**: Trained & evaluated on NVIDIA RTX 4090 (24GB VRAM)

## Intended Use
- Automated, high-precision crop disease identification and uncertainty-gated diagnostic triage in digital agriculture.
